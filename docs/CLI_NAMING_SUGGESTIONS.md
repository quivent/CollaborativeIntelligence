## Final Selection: CIA (Collaborative Intelligence Adaption)

**Three-Word Acronym Variants:**
- **ECI** - Evolving Collaborative Intelligence
- **ACI** - Adapting Collaborative Intelligence  
- **CIA** - Collaborative Intelligence Adaption

**Selected: CIA (Collaborative Intelligence Adaption)**

**Terminology Distinctions:**
- **Adaption**: An ongoing process by the system itself - the active, continuous process of self-modification
- **Adaptation**: Expression of a completed process - the result or state achieved through adaption
- **Adapting**: Reference to an external system adapting - when something else is doing the adapting

**Rationale:**
- Emphasizes humility by avoiding "Evolution" (which implies predetermined direction)
- Provides humor through the CIA acronym parallel
- Focuses on the system's own continuous process of self-improvement
- Maintains dignity by describing capability rather than subservience
- Captures the collaborative nature of the intelligence system
