# Sofia Development Team Specification

## Executive Summary

Our Sofia-based development team consists of 15 highly skilled professionals with extensive experience in C#, React, Unity, and C++ development. This cohesive team offers comprehensive expertise in Digital Twins, Building Information Modeling (BIM), Architecture, Engineering, and Construction (AEC), and Smart City solutions. With proven capabilities in 3D visualization, gaming development, and environmental impact analysis, our team delivers innovative, sustainable, and cost-effective solutions.

## Team Composition & Expertise

### Technical Proficiencies
- **C# Development**: Advanced enterprise application development, .NET Core expertise, microservices architecture
- **Frontend/React**: Modern UI/UX implementation, responsive design, state management, performance optimization
- **Unity Development**: Real-time 3D visualization, simulation environments, AR/VR implementation
- **C++ Engineering**: High-performance computing, systems programming, computational optimization

### Specialized Skills
- **Digital Twin Creation**: Complete implementation of virtual replicas for physical assets, systems, and processes
- **BIM Solutions**: Comprehensive building information modeling across all project phases
- **AEC Technology**: Specialized tools and applications for architecture, engineering, and construction workflows
- **Smart City Infrastructure**: Connected urban systems, IoT integration, data analytics for urban planning
- **Environmental Analysis**: Material property calculations, environmental footprint assessment, sustainability metrics
