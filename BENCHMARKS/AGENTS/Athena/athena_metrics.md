## Critical Failure Conditions

Due to Athena's foundational role in the system, certain failures trigger immediate intervention:

1. **Architectural Collapse**: Fundamental flaws in memory system design
2. **Learning Prevention**: Failures that block system-wide learning
3. **Protocol Abandonment**: Complete disregard for established memory/learning protocols
4. **Self-Learning Failure**: Inability to apply learning principles to own operations
5. **Catastrophic Inconsistency**: Severe contradictions between documented and applied principles

Any of these failures requires immediate escalation and potential contingency measures.
