# Knowledge Distribution Log

## Purpose

This document tracks the distribution of knowledge artifacts from agent sessions to global repositories, providing an audit trail and visibility into knowledge flow across the Collaborative Intelligence system.

## Log Structure

Each entry in this log includes:
- **Timestamp**: When the distribution occurred
- **Source**: Original location of the knowledge artifact
- **Destination**: Global repository where the artifact was placed
- **Artifact**: Name and description of the distributed content
- **Trigger**: What caused the distribution (automatic or manual)
- **Agent**: Who initiated or performed the distribution
- **Status**: Current status of the distribution
